# Garrison System In-Game Testing Guide

The garrison system powers content across **4 expansions**. This guide covers where to go, what to click, and what to expect.

---

## Warlords of Draenor — Your Garrison (Level 90-100)

### How You Get It
1. **Start the WoD intro** — Accept the quest at the Dark Portal in Blasted Lands (or use Chromie Time to set expansion to WoD). You'll be sent through the portal to Tanaan Jungle.
2. **Complete the Tanaan intro chain** (~30 min) — Fight through Tanaan, escape on a boat/through a cave.
3. **You arrive at your garrison site** — Horde lands in **Frostfire Ridge**, Alliance lands in **Shadowmoon Valley**. A quest tells you to plant your banner — that moment creates the garrison.

### Where It Is
- **Horde**: Southwest Frostfire Ridge (you'll see it on the map as a small fort icon)
- **Alliance**: Northern Shadowmoon Valley (same, fort icon on map)
- There's a **hearthstone ability** ("Garrison Hearthstone") in your spellbook to teleport back anytime

### What's Inside & What You Click

#### Town Hall (the big building in the center)
- Walk inside, that's your command center
- **Garrison Level**: Starts at Level 1. You upgrade it at the **Architect Table** (a drafting table inside/near the Town Hall). Upgrading unlocks more building plots.
  - Level 1: Small plots only
  - Level 2: Adds medium plots, more small plots (requires level 93 + 200 resources)
  - Level 3: Adds large plots (requires level 100 + 2000 resources + 5000 gold)

#### Architect Table (drafting table with blueprints)
- **Click it** to open the building UI
- Left side: Your garrison map with plot slots (small/medium/large)
- Right side: Available buildings you have blueprints for
- **Click a plot** → **Click a building** → **Place Building** button
- Buildings take real time to construct (varies by building, 30min to 24h)
- Once built, click the building plot again → **Activate** to start using it

#### Mission Table (war table with map)
- Manned by an NPC near the Town Hall
- **Talk to the NPC** or **click the table** to open the mission UI
- Left side: Available missions with rewards listed
- Right side: Your followers
- **Drag followers** onto mission slots → **Start Mission** button
- Missions take real time (30min to 24h). Come back later → **Complete Mission** → **Collect Rewards**
- Green percentage = success chance. Match follower abilities against mission threats to increase it.

#### Building-Specific Interactions

| Building | What You Do Inside |
|----------|-------------------|
| **Barracks** | Talk to NPC inside. At L2: +5 active follower slots. At L3: Assign a follower as **bodyguard** — they follow you in Draenor outdoor zones and fight alongside you |
| **Inn/Tavern** | Talk to NPC inside. At L2: Recruit one new follower per week with a trait you choose. Also unlocks treasure hunt missions on your mission table |
| **Salvage Yard** | No NPC interaction needed. After completing any mission, you automatically receive a **salvage crate** in your bags. Open it for random gear/items. Better crates at higher building levels |
| **War Mill / Dwarven Bunker** | Talk to NPC. Turn in Iron Horde Scraps for follower upgrade items (weapon/armor tokens). Also gives you a free bonus roll seal weekly |
| **Profession Buildings** (Alchemy Lab, Enchanter's Study, etc.) | Talk to the NPC inside → **Place Work Order** button. Give materials, receive crafted items after a timer. Each work order slot takes time. Building level = more work order slots |
| **Lumber Mill** | Lets you chop marked trees in Draenor zones for Garrison Resources |
| **Trading Post** | Trade crafting materials for Garrison Resources and vice versa. Gives access to an Auction House at L3 |
| **Storehouse** | Extra bank/void storage access inside your garrison. More tabs at higher levels |

#### Follower System
- Followers are recruited through **quests** in Draenor zones (quest reward adds them)
- Open follower list from the **Mission Table UI** → tab at top
- **Click a follower** to see their abilities, traits, item level
- **Right-click** → options to set favorite, deactivate, rename
- To **assign to a building**: Open building, click "Assign Follower" button, pick one

#### Shipyard (WoD 6.2 content)
- Unlocked at level 100 via quest chain in Tanaan Jungle
- Located on the **coast below your garrison** (new zone attached to garrison)
- Works like missions but with **ships** instead of followers
- **Click the naval mission table** → Send ships on naval missions
- Build ships at the **shipyard NPC** using Garrison Resources + Oil

## Legion — Class Order Hall (Level 98-110)

### How You Get It
1. **Start Legion content** — Go to Dalaran (Legion version, above Broken Isles)
2. **Pick up your artifact weapon quest** — This is class-specific, usually from a quest giver in Dalaran
3. **Complete the artifact scenario** — A short instanced story (20-30 min)
4. **You're sent to your Class Order Hall** — The quest chain takes you there automatically

### Where Each Class Hall Is

| Class | Location | How to Enter from Dalaran |
|-------|----------|--------------------------|
| **Warrior** | Skyhold (floating Valhalla) | Jump off Krasus' Landing (the flight platform) — a Valkyrie catches you |
| **Paladin** | Sanctum of Light (under Light's Hope) | Portal in the Paladin area of Dalaran |
| **Death Knight** | Acherus (floating necropolis) | Death Gate spell, or portal from Dalaran |
| **Mage** | Hall of the Guardian | Portal inside the Violet Citadel in Dalaran |
| **Warlock** | Dreadscar Rift (fel dimension) | Portal in the Dalaran Underbelly (sewers) |
| **Monk** | Temple of Five Dawns (Wandering Isle) | Portal near Dalaran flight master |
| **Druid** | Dreamgrove (in Val'sharah) | Portal in the Dalaran greenhouse area, or teleport spell |
| **Rogue** | Hall of Shadows | Sneak into the shop in Dalaran's alley (near the bookshop) |
| **Hunter** | Trueshot Lodge (Highmountain peak) | Talk to the eagle on Krasus' Landing |
| **Priest** | Netherlight Temple (floating in the Twisting Nether) | Portal in Dalaran near the priest trainer |
| **Shaman** | Heart of Azeroth (Maelstrom) | Portal on Dalaran's east side |
| **Demon Hunter** | Fel Hammer (on Mardum) | Portal on Krasus' Landing |

### What's Inside & What You Click

#### Scouting Map (Mission Table)
- A big map table, usually in the center of the hall
- **Click it** → Same UI as WoD mission table
- Send **Champions** (your Order Hall followers) + **Troops** on missions
- Champions = permanent followers you unlock via quests
- Troops = expendable units you train (they have durability, die after X missions)

#### Troop Recruiting
- Talk to the **Troop Trainer NPC** in your hall
- Spend Order Resources → Train a batch of troops
- Troops appear in your follower list with a durability counter

#### Class Hall Talent Tree
- **Click the UI element** (usually a book or altar in the hall)
- Opens a talent tree with ~6 tiers of choices
- Each tier: pick one of two options
- **Click a talent** → **Research** → Takes real time (hours to days)
- Talents give bonuses like: extra champion slots, shorter mission times, world quest bonuses

#### Class Campaign
- A quest chain that unlocks over weeks
- Gives you champions, unlocks hall features, and eventually your third artifact relic slot
- Just follow the quest markers inside your hall

## Battle for Azeroth — War Campaign (Level 110-120)

### How You Get It
1. **Reach your faction hub**: Horde → **Dazar'alor** (Zuldazar), Alliance → **Boralus** (Tiragarde Sound)
2. **Start the War Campaign** — Quest chain from your faction leader
3. **Establish footholds** on the enemy continent — 3 footholds in enemy zones

### Where It Is
- **Horde**: The **Great Seal** in Dazar'alor (top of the pyramid) — mission table is on the upper deck
- **Alliance**: **Harbormaster's Office** in Boralus — mission table is inside

### What's Inside & What You Click

#### Mission Table
- Same concept as before — **click the table**
- Send followers (War Campaign champions) on missions
- Rewards: reputation, war resources, Azerite, gear

#### War Campaign Quests
- Sequential quest chapters that unlock as you level and gain reputation
- Takes you to the opposite faction's continent to establish bases
- Unlocks new followers and mission table upgrades

**This is the simplest garrison type** — no buildings, no work orders, no talents. Just the mission table and followers.

## Shadowlands — Covenant Sanctum (Level 50-60)

### How You Get It
1. **Reach level 60** and complete the Shadowlands campaign through all 4 zones
2. **Choose your Covenant** — You pick Kyrian, Necrolord, Night Fae, or Venthyr
3. **You're taken to your Covenant Sanctum** — automatic quest teleport

### Where Each Sanctum Is

| Covenant | Location | Zone |
|----------|----------|------|
| **Kyrian** | Elysian Hold | Bastion (floating temple in the sky) |
| **Necrolord** | Seat of the Primus | Maldraxxus (bone fortress) |
| **Night Fae** | Heart of the Forest | Ardenweald (giant tree) |
| **Venthyr** | Sinfall | Revendreth (gothic crypt) |

Each sanctum has a flight path and you get a hearthstone-like teleport to it.

### What's Inside & What You Click

#### Adventure Table (Mission Table)
- **Click the table** in your sanctum
- This uses the **auto-combat system** — different from WoD/Legion!
- Your soulbind companions are placed on a **grid/board**
- Enemies are on the other side
- It plays out a **turn-based auto-battle** (the server simulates this)
- You position units, then hit **Start Adventure** and watch/skip the result

#### Soulbind Forge
- **Click the Soulbind NPC** or forge
- Opens the **Soulbind talent tree** (conduit system)
- Socket conduits (from drops) into soulbind nodes
- This maps to the garrison talent system in the code

#### Covenant Campaign
- 9-chapter quest chain released weekly
- Unlocks sanctum features, followers, cosmetics
- Follow quest markers in your sanctum

#### Sanctum Upgrades
- **Talk to the Upgrade NPC** in your sanctum
- Spend Reservoir Anima + Souls to upgrade covenant features:
  - **Transport Network**: Unlocks fast-travel portals in the covenant zone
  - **Anima Conductor**: Channel anima to unlock daily content in specific spots
  - **Covenant-specific feature**: Unique per covenant (e.g., Path of Ascension for Kyrian, Abominable Stitching for Necrolord, Queen's Conservatory for Night Fae, Ember Court for Venthyr)

## Quick Reference: What Feature Lives Where

| Feature | WoD | Legion | BfA | Shadowlands |
|---------|-----|--------|-----|-------------|
| **Mission Table** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes (auto-combat) |
| **Followers/Champions** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Troops (expendable)** | No | Yes | No | Yes |
| **Building Placement** | Yes | No | No | No |
| **Work Orders** | Yes | No | No | No |
| **Talent Tree** | No | Yes | No | Yes (Soulbinds) |
| **Bodyguard** | Yes | No | No | No |
| **Shipyard** | Yes | No | No | No |
| **Invasions** | Yes | No | No | No |
| **Party Visit** | Yes | No | No | No |
| **Trophies/Monuments** | Yes | No | No | No |

The **WoD garrison** is by far the most feature-rich and is where the majority of testing should focus. Legion and Shadowlands add their own twists (troops, auto-combat, soulbinds), while BfA is the simplest.

## GM Commands for Testing

```
# Give yourself garrison resources for each expansion
.addcurrency 824 10000     # Garrison Resources (WoD)
.addcurrency 1220 10000    # Order Resources (Legion)
.addcurrency 1560 10000    # War Resources (BfA)
.addcurrency 1813 10000    # Reservoir Anima (Shadowlands)

# Test garrison invasions (WoD only, must be in garrison)
.garrison invasion start 0  # Iron Horde invasion
.garrison invasion start 1  # Shadow Council invasion
.garrison invasion start 2  # Botani invasion
.garrison invasion score    # Check current score
.garrison invasion stop     # End invasion early
```
